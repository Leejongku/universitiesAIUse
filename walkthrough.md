# University AI Research Data Compilation Walkthrough

Due to Google Custom Search API quota limitations (100 free queries per day), I performed direct research and manual data compilation for 18 major Korean universities.

## Methodology

1.  **Direct Research**: Performed targeted web searches for each of the 18 universities.
2.  **Information Extraction**: Identified:
    *   Generative AI adoption status and platforms.
    *   Official AI chatbot services (Student vs. Faculty).
    *   AI usage guidelines and policies.
3.  **Data Structuring**: Compiled the findings into a structured format compatible with the original script's output.
4.  **Report Generation**: Generated a multi-sheet Excel report using Python and Pandas.

## Key Findings

*   **Platform Integration**: Many universities (SNU, Kyung Hee, Sungsil, Dongguk) are moving towards integrated platforms that offer multiple LLMs (GPT, Claude, Gemini) to their members.
*   **Specialized Chatbots**: Universities are deploying chatbots for specific needs, such as SNU's 'SNU AIChat', Korea's 'KUChat', and Sookmyung's 'AI 눈송'.
*   **Instructional Innovation**: Dankook University has introduced 'AI Personas' (AI Professors) as part of their 'AX-EL' education model.
